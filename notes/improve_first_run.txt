timeliness.)

claims active. (Keeps the active belief set clean and avoids wrong active beliefs.)

many weeks of confirmation. (Earlier confidence on the true driver improves the timeliness score.)

Diagnosis: The run scored well: it found the true driver (distribution) and ended with no wrong, non-causal, or duplicate beliefs. The remaining loss 
is timeliness. The agent opened three week-2 hypotheses at 0.50 and spent that week on a false price match. It only reached 0.7 in week 4, one week 
after the driver first moved in week 3.
  • edited rule: Each week, compare every driver metric's percentage change with the outcome's change in that same week, and open a belief only for 
drivers that actually changed. Do not open hypotheses for drivers that were flat. Judge each week's move on its own metric rather than carrying 
forward last week's explanation. (Avoids wasted speculative beliefs on flat drivers and keeps weekly attribution current.)
  • edited rule: When a driver's move matches the outcome in size and timing and no other driver moved, start the belief at 0.6 or higher and raise it
by at least +0.2 on each further match, reaching 0.7 on the first confirming observation after creation. (Speeds confidence to the 0.7 threshold, 
improving timeliness.)
  • edited rule: A single-week match is a candidate, not a conclusion. Confirm it by checking that the other drivers were flat that week. If a driver 
moves in the same direction again in a later week, treat that as confirmation even when the size is partly cushioned by a secondary driver. (Keeps the
confirmation check while allowing repeated matches to lift confidence quickly despite offsetting drivers.)
